How a batch works
  1. Commit — your order commits as a hash with funds escrowed. No one can see side, size, or price. You can cancel it any time before the commit window closes — escrow and bond come back in full.
  2. Reveal — after the commit window closes, you reveal. The program checks it against your commitment; your bond returns.
  3. Clear — the keeper proposes one uniform price; the program verifies it's the maximal cross inside the band.
  4. Settle — you're filled at that one price (0.75% fee on filled size only), or fully refunded if the batch doesn't cross. Miss your reveal and only the 0.25 SOL bond is forfeit — your escrow always returns.
